Begin Viral Magic OS — Phase 1E: Business Growth Assessment Engine.

Follow the approved Viral Magic Development Playbook:

- Keep the solution simple.
- Reuse existing Phase 1D diagnostic data and components.
- Avoid complex workflows unless required.
- Minimize database changes, API calls, maintenance, and Replit credit usage.
- Preserve all working functionality from Phases 1A through 1D.1.
- Build only what is required to interpret the assessment and make it useful for consulting.

Do not begin Phase 1F or Phase 1G.

==========================================================
1. PHASE 1E OBJECTIVE
==========================================================

Turn completed diagnostic scores into a clear executive-level Business Growth Assessment.

The system must explain:

- What the scores mean
- What is working
- What is vulnerable
- What is creating risk
- Which problems require immediate attention
- Which areas can be monitored
- What the business should focus on next

This phase interprets existing data.

Do not create a second diagnostic form.

==========================================================
2. NAVIGATION AND VERSION
==========================================================

Keep the existing sidebar modules.

Do not add unnecessary new navigation.

Add a working “Growth Assessment” tab or section inside each completed Diagnostic Results page.

Update the release label consistently to:

Viral Magic OS
Version 1.0 Release Candidate
Phase 1E — Growth Assessment

Remove outdated Phase 1A or Phase 1D labels where they remain.

==========================================================
3. DATABASE APPROACH
==========================================================

Reuse existing diagnostic, diagnostic-version, score, recommendation, client, project, and activity tables wherever practical.

Add only the minimum database fields or table required to store an assessment interpretation.

The Growth Assessment must be linked to:

- Client
- Optional project
- Diagnostic
- Diagnostic version

Store:

- Assessment status
- Business Health Score
- Health rating
- Strength summary
- Vulnerability summary
- Risk summary
- Growth-opportunity summary
- Quick-win summary
- Strategic-focus summary
- Consultant notes
- Reviewed by
- Reviewed at
- Approved by
- Approved at
- Created at
- Updated at

Do not duplicate all diagnostic scores inside a new table.

==========================================================
4. ASSESSMENT INTERPRETATION
==========================================================

Use deterministic, rule-based interpretation.

Do not use OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, or another external AI API in Phase 1E.

Interpret results using:

- Business Health Score
- Health rating
- Category performance
- Business impact
- Urgency
- Priority score
- Severity
- Resolution status
- Existing rule-based recommendations

The Administrator must be able to edit all generated interpretation text.

Store the original system-generated text separately from the final Administrator-approved text when practical.

==========================================================
5. EXECUTIVE ASSESSMENT SUMMARY
==========================================================

Create a clear executive summary containing:

BUSINESS HEALTH

- Score from 0 to 100
- Rating:
  - Strong
  - Stable
  - Vulnerable
  - At Risk
  - Critical
- Short plain-English explanation

TOP STRENGTHS

Identify up to three categories with:

- Strong current performance
- Meaningful business impact
- Healthy or Monitor severity

For each strength show:

- Category
- Why it is a strength
- How to protect or maintain it

TOP VULNERABILITIES

Identify up to five categories that are weak but may not yet be the highest emergency.

For each show:

- Category
- Current performance
- Priority score
- Severity
- What the weakness means

PRIMARY RISKS

Identify High and Critical bottlenecks.

For each show:

- Category
- Why it matters
- Likely operational or growth consequence
- Evidence entered during the diagnostic

Do not invent financial losses or guaranteed outcomes.

==========================================================
6. QUICK WINS
==========================================================

Identify up to three Quick Wins using simple rules.

A Quick Win should generally have:

- Moderate or High priority
- Clear recommended action
- Lower implementation complexity
- A realistic ability to show progress quickly

Because Phase 1D does not yet store complete implementation-complexity data, use conservative category-based defaults that remain editable.

Examples of likely Quick Win categories may include:

- Speed to Lead
- Follow-Up
- Lead Capture
- Reputation and Reviews
- Website calls to action
- Basic Reporting
- Simple Automation

For each Quick Win display:

- Action
- Reason
- Suggested owner
- Suggested time horizon:
  - Immediate
  - 7 Days
  - 30 Days
- Expected business improvement stated cautiously

Do not promise revenue increases.

==========================================================
7. STRATEGIC PRIORITIES
==========================================================

Identify the top three to five Strategic Priorities.

This is not yet the full Phase 1F recommendation order.

For Phase 1E, classify priorities into:

- Immediate Attention
- Near-Term Improvement
- Strategic Development
- Monitor and Maintain

Use the existing scores and severity.

Display:

- Category
- Classification
- Why it belongs there
- Current performance
- Business impact
- Urgency
- Priority score
- Existing recommended action

==========================================================
8. GROWTH OPPORTUNITIES
==========================================================

Create a Growth Opportunities section.

Identify opportunities from categories where:

- Performance is weak or moderate
- Business impact is high
- Improvement could support growth, efficiency, visibility, conversion, retention, or scalability

Group opportunities under:

- Revenue and Lead Growth
- Sales and Conversion
- Customer Experience and Retention
- Operations and Efficiency
- Technology and Automation
- Reporting and Scalability

Only show groups with relevant findings.

For each opportunity include:

- Opportunity title
- Related diagnostic category
- Business reason
- Suggested direction
- Priority classification

==========================================================
10. EDITING AND APPROVAL
==========================================================

The Administrator can:

- Edit generated wording
- Add consultant notes
- Save a draft
- Submit for review
- Approve the final assessment
- Reopen an approved assessment with an audit-log entry

Statuses:

- Draft
- Awaiting Review
- Approved
- Superseded
- Archived

Approved historical assessments should not be silently changed.

If the linked diagnostic receives a new version, allow a new Growth Assessment to be generated for that version without overwriting the previous one.
