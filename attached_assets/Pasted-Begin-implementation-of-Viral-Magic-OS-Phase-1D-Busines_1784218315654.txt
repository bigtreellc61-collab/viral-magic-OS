# 4. SCORING INPUTS

For every category collect:

CURRENT PERFORMANCE:

- Range: 0 to 10
- 0 means completely broken or nonexistent
- 10 means excellent and consistently performing

BUSINESS IMPACT:

- Range: 1 to 5
- 1 means minimal business impact
- 5 means major revenue, operational, or strategic impact

URGENCY:

- Range: 1 to 5
- 1 means low urgency
- 5 means immediate attention required

Also collect:

- Evidence
- Observations
- Notes
- Recommended action

Use sliders, number inputs, or a combination with clear labels.

Do not allow values outside the defined ranges.

# 5. SCORING FORMULAS

Calculate on the server and display live previews on the client.

PERFORMANCE GAP:

10 minus Current Performance

PRIORITY SCORE:

Performance Gap × Business Impact × Urgency

Possible range:

0 to 250

SEVERITY LEVELS:

- 0–39: Healthy
- 40–79: Monitor
- 80–129: Moderate
- 130–189: High
- 190–250: Critical

Store the calculated values with each score record.

Never trust client-side calculations alone.

Recalculate server-side before saving or completing a diagnostic.

# 6. BUSINESS HEALTH SCORE

Calculate an overall Business Health Score from 0 to 100.

Use this Phase 1D formula:

For each active category:

Category Health Percentage =
Current Performance ÷ 10 × 100

Weighted Category Health =
Category Health Percentage × Business Impact

Overall Business Health Score =
Sum of Weighted Category Health ÷ Sum of Business Impact

Round to the nearest whole number for display but preserve useful precision in storage.

HEALTH RATING:

- 85–100: Strong
- 70–84: Stable
- 55–69: Vulnerable
- 40–54: At Risk
- 0–39: Critical

Do not create a false financial revenue-leak dollar estimate in Phase 1D.

We do not yet have enough validated client financial data for credible dollar-loss calculations.

# 7. OVERALL PRIORITY SCORE

Calculate:

Average Priority Score =
Sum of active category priority scores ÷ Number of active categories

Also display:

- Highest category priority score
- Number of Moderate bottlenecks
- Number of High bottlenecks
- Number of Critical bottlenecks

Do not mix the 0–100 Health Score with the 0–250 category Priority Score.

Use clear labels and tooltips so the Administrator understands the difference.

# 8. RULE-BASED RECOMMENDATION ENGINE

Do not use an external AI API.

Generate a draft recommendation for every category using:

- Category
- Current performance
- Business impact
- Urgency
- Severity
- Evidence
- Observations

The rule system should create practical draft recommendations.

Examples of recommendation logic:

LEAD GENERATION:

- Low performance and High/Critical severity:
  Recommend establishing a measurable lead-generation system, clarifying the offer, defining the target customer, testing outbound and inbound channels, and tracking cost per qualified lead.

SPEED TO LEAD:

- Low performance and High/Critical severity:
  Recommend immediate lead notifications, automated acknowledgment, response-time targets, CRM routing, and follow-up accountability.

FOLLOW-UP:

- Low performance and High/Critical severity:
  Recommend a structured multi-touch follow-up sequence using email, phone, SMS where appropriate, task reminders, and pipeline-stage ownership.

SALES CONVERSION:

- Low performance and High/Critical severity:
  Recommend reviewing qualification, sales scripts, proposal process, objection handling, follow-up, and conversion tracking.

WEBSITE PERFORMANCE:

- Low performance and High/Critical severity:
  Recommend reviewing mobile speed, message clarity, calls to action, trust signals, lead capture, analytics, and conversion paths.

AUTOMATION:

- Low performance and High/Critical severity:
  Recommend identifying repetitive manual work, documenting the workflow, selecting high-impact automation opportunities, and adding monitoring and fallback procedures.

TECHNOLOGY INTEGRATION:

- Low performance and High/Critical severity:
  Recommend mapping the technology stack, identifying duplicate tools and disconnected data, prioritizing integrations, and creating a single source of truth.

REPORTING AND ANALYTICS:

- Low performance and High/Critical severity:
  Recommend defining key operating metrics, assigning data ownership, creating a dashboard, setting review cadence, and eliminating vanity metrics.

OPERATIONAL EFFICIENCY:

- Low performance and High/Critical severity:
  Recommend documenting core workflows, identifying delays and rework, standardizing procedures, assigning ownership, and automating repetitive steps.

SCALABILITY:

- Low performance and High/Critical severity:
  Recommend reducing owner dependency, documenting processes, creating repeatable systems, improving reporting, and identifying capacity constraints.

Create concise maintenance recommendations for Healthy and Monitor categories.

Requirements:

- Store the system-generated draft separately
- Allow Administrator editing
- Allow Approve, Reject, or Mark Implemented
- Preserve the original draft
- Never overwrite approved final text with a rerun

Keep rules modular and editable later.

# 19. ARCHIVE, RESTORE, AND DELETE

ARCHIVE:

- Set archived_at
- Set status to Archived
- Preserve versions, scores, and recommendations
- Hide from default lists
- Log activity

RESTORE:

- Clear archived_at
- Require a valid restored status
- Log activity

PERMANENT DELETE:

- Only archived diagnostics
- Strong confirmation required
- Explain that all versions, scores, and recommendations will be removed
- Use a transaction
- Protect diagnostics referenced by future modules
- Log before deletion where technically possible

Do not make permanent deletion a primary action.
