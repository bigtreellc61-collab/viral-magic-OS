==================================================
1. PHASE 1F PURPOSE
==================================================

Phase 1F must convert an approved or completed Business Growth Assessment into a clear, prioritized set of business recommendations.

Phase 1E answers:

“What is happening in this business?”

Phase 1F must answer:

“What should the business fix first, why does it matter, and what outcome should it create?”

The engine must be:

- Deterministic
- Rule-based
- Explainable
- Editable by an administrator or consultant
- Auditable
- Reproducible
- Independent from third-party AI services

Recommendations must be grounded in:

- Diagnostic category scores
- Severity
- Performance
- Priority score
- Business impact
- Growth Assessment risks
- Vulnerabilities
- Quick wins
- Strategic priorities
- Growth opportunities
- Existing recommendation text from Phase 1D, when available

Do not invent unsupported business facts.

==================================================
2. CORE WORKFLOW
==================================================

Required workflow:

Completed Diagnostic
→ Growth Assessment
→ Approved Growth Assessment
→ Generate Solution Recommendations
→ Draft
→ Awaiting Review
→ Approved
→ Reopen if changes are required
→ Superseded or Archived

Default requirement:

A Solution Recommendation Plan should normally be created from an approved Growth Assessment.

If the system allows creation from an unapproved assessment, show a clear warning and require administrator confirmation.

Only one active recommendation plan should exist for the same assessment version.

Generating a new plan from a newer assessment version should not overwrite an older approved plan.

Older plans must remain available for audit history.

3. RECOMMENDATION OUTPUT
==================================================

Each Solution Recommendation Plan must contain the following sections.

A. Executive Recommendation

Create a concise system-generated summary explaining:

- Overall business condition
- Most important risk
- Most promising growth opportunity
- Recommended focus for the next 90 days
- Why the recommended order matters

Store the original system-generated version separately from the editable administrator version.

B. Top Priority Recommendations

Generate the top 3 to 5 recommendations.

Each recommendation must include:

- Rank
- Recommendation title
- Business domain
- Source assessment category
- Problem statement
- Why it matters
- Recommended outcome
- Business impact score
- Urgency score
- Effort estimate
- Confidence level
- Recommended timeframe
- Suggested owner
- Dependency notes
- Success metric
- Current status
- Administrator notes

C. Recommended Actions

Each priority recommendation must contain action steps grouped into:

- Immediate
- Within 7 Days
- Within 30 Days
- Within 60–90 Days

Each action step must include:

- Action title
- Description
- Suggested owner
- Time horizon
- Expected outcome
- Success metric
- Completion status
- Sort order

D. Business Impact Summary

Summarize expected influence across:

- Revenue growth
- Lead generation
- Sales conversion
- Customer retention
- Customer experience
- Operational efficiency
- Technology and automation
- Reporting and visibility
- Scalability

Use a consistent impact scale, such as:

1 — Minimal
2 — Low
3 — Moderate
4 — High
5 — Transformational

E. Dependencies and Sequencing

Identify prerequisite relationships.

Examples:

- Lead capture before increasing advertising
- CRM setup before advanced automation
- Response process before scaling lead volume
- Tracking before optimization
- Offer clarity before funnel expansion
- Sales follow-up before traffic growth
- Standard operating process before delegation

The system must prevent circular dependencies.

F. Consultant Notes

Provide an editable free-text section for:

- Client context
- Exceptions
- Strategic judgment
- Risks not captured by the engine
- Presentation notes
- Follow-up questions

G. Approval

Include:

- Draft status
- Submit for Review
- Approve
- Reopen
- Archive
- Approval history
- Reviewed by and reviewed at
- Approved by and approved at
- Activity log

5. PRIORITY SCORING
==================================================

Create a transparent priority-scoring method.

Suggested components:

- Severity score
- Business impact score
- Urgency score
- Performance gap
- Quick-win potential
- Dependency importance
- Confidence score

Create named constants at the top of the recommendation engine so the thresholds and weights can be changed later without rewriting the logic.

Suggested initial formula:

priority score =
severity weight
+ business impact weight
+ urgency weight
+ performance gap weight
+ quick-win bonus
+ dependency bonus
- effort penalty

Normalize the final score to 0–100.

Suggested classification:

90–100 — Critical Priority
80–89 — High Priority
65–79 — Important
50–64 — Planned Improvement
0–49 — Monitor

Do not rely only on the Phase 1E priority score.

The new score must remain explainable.

For each recommendation, store a scoring explanation showing which inputs produced the score.
